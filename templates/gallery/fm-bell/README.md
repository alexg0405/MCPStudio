# FM Bell

An instrument: play notes and a bell rings. One sine wave (the modulator) bends the pitch of another (the carrier), which
is called FM.

- **Ratio**: whole numbers (1, 2, 3) sound like an organ; fractions such as 3.5 or 5.4 sound like metal.
- **Brightness**: how hard the modulator bends the note.
- **Ring time**: how long the bell takes to fade; the bright part fades about twice as fast.
- **Release**: how quickly a note is damped when you let go.
